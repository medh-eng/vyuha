VYUHA · व्यूह — Privacy policy

Last updated 17 November 2026

VYUHA is a free strategy game made by medh-eng. This policy covers the Android app and the web version at medh-eng.github.io/vyuha.

What we collect

Nothing. VYUHA has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. It does not ask for your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files. We do not collect, sell or share any personal data.

What stays on your phone

Your player name, settings, progress, saved battles and Daily results are kept only on your own device, in the app's local storage. They never leave the device unless you choose to export a backup or share a result. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data, deletes them.

Sharing

When you tap Share or Challenge a friend, the game prepares a short text or picture and hands it to the app you pick, such as WhatsApp. What happens next is governed by that app's own policy. VYUHA sends nothing anywhere unless you tap Share.

Internet

The game plays fully offline. The Android app uses the internet only to open a link you tap (for example a share to WhatsApp). The web version downloads the game files from GitHub Pages, which, like any web server, may keep standard access logs; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

VYUHA has no chat, no in-app purchases, no ads and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: open an issue at github.com/medh-eng/vyuha/issues or write to the contact email shown on the game's Google Play page.
